I designed and launched an iOS polling feature with an annual revenue target of $1M. It generated seven times that target in its first year.

Overview

Engineering tied to a clear business goal.

The feature sat inside a large consumer application used by millions of people. Product decisions, performance, advertising systems and user experience all affected the result.

The assignment was not simply to make a poll render on screen. It was to carry an experience from design through implementation and release while keeping the product objective visible throughout the work.

My role

From product intent to production.

I worked on the iOS implementation and carried the feature through design and release. That meant treating the surrounding product and commercial systems as part of the engineering problem.

  • Translated the polling concept into an iOS experience suitable for a high-traffic consumer application.
  • Balanced interface quality, application performance and advertising requirements.
  • Owned the work through implementation and production release rather than stopping at a prototype.

Seven times the target.

Takeaway

The code mattered. So did shipping the right experience.

The outcome came from understanding the product goal, working within a complex consumer application and getting the experience into production. Strong implementation was necessary, but its value appeared only when it served the complete product.
