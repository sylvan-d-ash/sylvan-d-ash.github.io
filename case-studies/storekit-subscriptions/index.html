Tinker · StoreKit 2 subscriptions

One subscription lifecycle. Two production apps.

I implemented the iOS StoreKit 2 work for premium feature access in LendPal and LinkNexus, including purchase recovery and changing entitlement states.

Challenge

Premium access has more than one happy path.

LendPal and LinkNexus needed production subscription handling for premium features. A purchase is only the beginning: customers restore purchases, transactions update later, and access can change after refunds or revocations.

The application needed one dependable view of entitlement status across those customer states.

My work

The client-side subscription lifecycle.

My scope covered the customer-facing iOS lifecycle, from the initial purchase through ongoing transaction changes.

StoreKit 2 · Customer and entitlement states

01Purchase product
02Resolve entitlement
03Unlock premium state
04Restore previous purchases
OngoingObserve refunds, revocations and updates
  • Product purchases and restoration of previous purchases.
  • Entitlement state used by the application to control premium access.
  • Refund and revocation handling.
  • Ongoing changes observed through Transaction.updates.
  • Product and design collaboration around the customer flows.
Scope

Clear boundaries make technical claims useful.

Both applications received production StoreKit 2 handling across their main customer states, including purchase recovery and changes to entitlement status.

Client-side responsibility

My work was on the iOS client. It did not include Stripe, App Store server notifications, server-side signed-transaction verification or reconciliation.

Outcome

Production subscription access across both products.

LendPal and LinkNexus gained consistent client-side subscription behaviour for purchasing, restoring access and responding to later transaction changes.
